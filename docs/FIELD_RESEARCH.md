# Field validation plan — real interviews, not fabricated evidence

## Purpose
The hackathon brief asks teams to take the official problem into the field. The person doing the interview should record interview date, contact consent and anonymized notes. Do not manufacture users, measurements or testimonials.

## Ask an SHG leader
1. How did you divide your latest bulk order, and who made the decision?
2. How do you estimate raw material, working time, waste and actual profit?
3. Which members could not finish on time? Why?
4. What happens when products are rejected after making them?
5. How do members confirm payments today? Any disagreements?
6. Would a member prefer fewer difficult pieces or more easy ones at the same rate?
7. What would convince you to use a new phone-based system?

## Ask at least two members separately
1. How do you know how many pieces were credited to your name?
2. Would you trust an assignment if the app explained its reasons aloud?
3. What skills/health/family commitments affect how much you can take on?
4. How should quality rejections and rework affect pay?
5. What languages and input method are most comfortable?
6. How can you dispute or correct an incorrect record?

## Observe, do not assume
- Real order sizes, due dates and difficulty by product type.
- Real working hours/production capacity spread.
- Existing member allocation patterns and norms of fairness.
- Time it takes to make a quote; differences between projected and actual costs.
- Whether a member with no smartphone can receive and verify the work/pay record.
